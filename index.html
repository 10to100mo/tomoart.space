ABOUT

TOMO is a painter based in Tokyo. Working primarily with oil on canvas and panel, her practice investigates the relationship between material and light—how pigment, applied and removed across many sessions, holds time within its surface.

Her work has been shown at institutions and galleries in Japan and internationally. She received a BFA from Tokyo University of the Arts.
